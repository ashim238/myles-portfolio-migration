## Claim audit

### Fresh Greens

**PASS**

- The opening is explicitly personal. It describes fear of a police stop or car
  trouble on unfamiliar South Jersey backroads rather than presenting that
  experience as a universal fact.
- The research section names the six-interview boundary before describing the
  patterns participants shared.
- The current prototype and the intended trust model are kept separate. A
  firsthand report remains visible as one account. Automatic route ranking is
  described as a future thresholded behavior, with slower corroboration for
  sparsely populated areas.
- The page does not claim that the prototype makes a route or driver safe.
- The Green Book lineage is framed as a design precedent for using the tools
  available in the present, not as an equivalence claim.

The Fresh Greens repository's historical thesis audit independently supports
the six-interview basis, community knowledge model, route re-ranking behavior,
and distinction between shipped and aspirational surfaces
(`docs/archive/thesis-coverage-2026-05-30.md:377-404`). The current portfolio
page remains more conservative than that product audit.

### Navi

**PASS**

- The 14-response sample is named rather than generalized into broad resident
  validation.
- The graduate-studio concept, team context, and later solo rebuild are
  distinguished.
- The page explains the resident and stakeholder stakes before showing the
  service model.

### UnderstandingFAFSA

**PASS**

- The narrative connects a 120-plus-page audit to explicit editorial rules.
- The founder workflow and the design response are separated from the public
  information architecture problem.
- The 102 KB image constraint is concrete.
- The 52.6 percent figure excludes MPP programs and is not presented as proof
  that the redesign caused the measured result.

### TikTok

**PASS**

- The page states the product, role, team, three-template system, and one shipped
  result without inflating the scope.
- The American Eagle relationship remains described as indirect.
- The GCL feedback sentence appears in both the iteration story and the closing
  evidence layer. This is a minor repetition, not a credibility or comprehension
  problem, so it remains unchanged under the agreed fix threshold.

### Play

**PASS**

- The active-lab framing is accurate. Every experiment exposes its question,
  medium, state, latest change, next step, and update date.
- Loom is presented as an active experiment rather than a finished product.
- The working notes describe observable behavior rather than implying adoption
  or impact.
