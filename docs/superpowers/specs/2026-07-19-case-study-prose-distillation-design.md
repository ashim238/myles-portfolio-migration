# Case-study prose distillation

**Date:** 2026-07-19
**Surface:** Fresh Greens, Navi, and UnderstandingFAFSA case studies

## Goal

Reduce localized reading friction without thinning the design-process story. Keep every process chapter, proof-bearing artifact, number, caveat, tool, and ownership claim. TikTok is already concise and stays unchanged.

## Voice contract

- Write in Myles's direct, conversational first-person voice.
- Prefer concrete decisions and actions over abstract system language.
- Keep contractions and natural sentence variation.
- Do not add em dashes, semicolons, hype, canned transitions, or slogan-like closers.
- Treat chapter titles as part of the prose. They must name a process stage clearly and still sound like Myles.
