## Goal

The family contains 16 original late-1990s desktop pixel illustrations. Every concept has independently authored 16, 24, and 32-unit SVG masters. No tier is generated by scaling another tier. The icons must preserve a literal noun, stable hierarchy, object-specific depth, and native-size legibility without copying a proprietary asset or brand glyph.

## Portrait likeness contract

Start and About Myles depict Myles, not a generic user. `public/logomark.svg` remains the canonical portrait-mark reference. The pixel adaptations preserve dark skin, long locs with tapered sides, glasses, a moustache, separate chin hair, and no continuous full-beard mass. At 16 units, the portrait noun takes precedence. At 24 units, the defining likeness cues resolve. At 32 units, the complete approved likeness cues must remain visible without weakening the portrait silhouette.

## Collision rules

- Fresh Greens owns a compact street-block road-map network with one highlighted unbranched three-turn route and distinct endpoint markers, not a magic wand, music note, folded map, notebook band, circuit, or landscape. Navi owns a separate pin-and-store destination stack.
- E-mail is a sealed message. UnderstandingFAFSA is a wide folded printed newsletter with paper depth, a tangible horizontal crease, and editorial print anatomy, not browser chrome, a browser window, flat screen, dashboard tile, framed web surface, envelope, folded map, mountain, or letter.
- Reminders is a checkbox/checkmark checklist, not a calendar. Notes is a folded memo, not a checklist.
- Display Properties is a CRT with in-screen controls, not a television. Reset Desktop is one connected open restart C-loop with a compact directional wedge and intentional gap at every tier, never a device, fuel pump, instant camera, chain link, paperclip, pencil, alert flag, transfer control, cable, pointer, telephone handset, or alarm clock. `reset desktop` is an action label, not a depicted device.
- Selected Work is a project dossier with two cards, not a photo landscape or photo folder.
- TikTok Catalog is a handled shopping bag, never a wastebasket, basket, small tote, catalog page, social-app mark, or music note.
- Loose Parts remains a literal non-branded compact 2+1 stack of three independent wooden construction blocks with square 3×3/5×5/7×7 front faces, compact six-point outlines, lit upper-left planes, dark right planes, and upper-face highlights at 24px and 32px, never books, boots, people, bottles, clothing, a stair, bar chart, proprietary toy-brick system, food, or furniture.

## Pixel construction grammar

- Exact viewBoxes: `0 0 16 16`, `0 0 24 24`, and `0 0 32 32`.
- Integer-coordinate filled `path`, `rect`, and `polygon` primitives only, with `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`.
- Binary transparency, filled contour bands, square or stair-stepped corners, and a transparent one-pixel perimeter.
- No fractional coordinates, smooth curves, transforms, strokes, filters, masks, clipping, gradients, opacity, translucent shadows, or rounded geometry.
- Consistent upper-left lighting and darker right/lower planes, but object-specific depth rather than a shared extrusion wrapper.
- No text, trademark, social-media mark, Windows flag, LEGO stud pattern, or copied proprietary glyph.

## Exact topology contract

- Navi 24px and 32px are exactly two opaque components, preserving a one-row gap between pin and storefront.
- Resume 24px and 32px have only the exact compact paperclip openings.
- Every remaining master, including Loose Parts and all three Reset Desktop tiers, is one opaque component with no enclosed transparency. Reset Desktop has no enclosed transparency at any tier.

The shared fill-integrity suite rasterizes all 48 masters and mutation-tests every allowed exception. It passed 53/53 for the current candidate.
